# Background photo credit

`scene.webp` is the one backdrop used on every page. Photo from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)
(free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)):
https://unsplash.com/photos/8c63bf9e7ee3 (a cosy living room by a window), re-encoded to WebP at 1920px wide.

To change it, replace `scene.webp` with another dark, low-detail WebP of about 1920px. If text gets hard to
read, raise the darkening in `.scene-grade` in `src/app/globals.css`.
